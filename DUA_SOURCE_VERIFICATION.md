# Dua Source Verification Report

Audit date: 2026-11-27

## Scope and method

Reviewed all 38 unique Dua records across 7 categories. The Arabic wording was compared with each cited Quran.com Ayah or the linked Sunnah.com collection record. English and Urdu meanings were checked for semantic alignment with the Arabic and cited source; they are editorial renderings, not separately certified translations. Hadith grades are attributed to the named collection/compiler or edition/grader shown in the record, not presented as a single consensus grade. Quran verses have no hadith authenticity grade.

All reference links shown below were opened and checked. Quran text checks used Quran.com. Hadith text and grading checks used the cited original collection entry on Sunnah.com, including the displayed Darussalam/Al-Albani grading or compiler wording where available.

## Limitation retained on the website

- **allahumma-rahmataka-arju**: The Hadith.net listing attributes Da'if to Zubair Ali Zai, but does not provide the underlying primary citation. The website states this explicitly as unverified while retaining Al-Albani's traceable Hasan-in-chain grade.
- **allahumma-minal-barasi**: Tohed reports both Al-Albani's Sahih and Zubair Ali Zai's Da'if grade, citing Anwar al-Sahifa, p. 61. The website names both scholars and states they are differing assessments, not consensus.

No Dua was removed. No Quranic grade was assigned.
